## Which funding route fits which buyer

If you already hold USDT, the cheapest path is boring: deposit it, trade PEPE/USDT or convert, pay 0.1% or less. Nothing beats that.

For a one-off purchase of a few hundred dollars with no crypto on hand, P2P or card is the honest choice. Paying 1–5% to get in is annoying, but it costs less than waiting three business days for a bank transfer while trying to time a meme coin entry.

Above roughly $1,000, bank transfer starts to earn its slowness. On $5,000, a card at 3% is $150 thrown away; SEPA or a wire is usually a rounding error by comparison. The friction is real, so decide in advance whether you're buying today or buying this week.

## Storing PEPE without losing it

Leaving PEPE in your Gate spot wallet is the default and it's fine if you plan to trade it. If you're holding long-term, self-custody moves the risk of platform failure onto your own key management — which is a genuine risk, not a theoretical one. A lost seed phrase is unrecoverable, and no support desk can help you.

PEPE is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum, so mainstream wallets that handle ERC-20 assets work. Two hard rules when you move it:

1. Check the network name and the receiving address character by character. Sending an ERC-20 token to a wallet expecting a different chain's format is not a recoverable mistake.
2. Send a small test amount first and wait for it to arrive before sending the rest. Gate's own guide says this, and it's the single cheapest insurance in crypto.

Also remember that on-chain transfers are irreversible once broadcast. There's no chargeback to appeal to.

## Mistakes that cost beginners money

**Buying the wrong PEPE.** Ticker collisions are rampant. Price aggregator pages for unrelated tokens also branded PEPE show market caps under a million dollars and holder counts in the dozens — those are different assets entirely, sometimes with liquidity so thin you can't exit. Cross-check market cap and 24-hour volume against the numbers you expect before you buy anything.

**Treating the channel fee as the trading fee.** Covered above; it's the biggest single line item for most small purchases.

**Letting the quote expire.** In the card flow you have around a minute. If the page has sat open while you fetched your card, refresh it.

**Buying before KYC.** You'll discover the routes are missing and assume the platform is broken.

**Chasing VIP tiers for basis points.** The savings between adjacent low tiers are microscopic. Volume-weighted tier maths rewards traders, not buyers.

**Market-buying a large order into thin depth.** Check the pair's recent volume first, or split the order.
